    Future scope

    From a lab-validated engine to a deployable covert-communication and media-forensics platform.

    AIDE already does both jobs on one CPU: it moves encrypted messages through hostile, lossy channels, and it screens intercepted media for hidden data. This page sets out the problems that motivated it, the engineering challenges solved along the way, the ones still open, and the plan to close them.

    Where this technology can go

    The same core — adaptive embedding, authenticated encryption, error correction, tamper localization and steganalysis — serves well beyond defence.

    Responsible use. Steganography is dual-use. That is why AIDE ships the detector alongside the embedder, publishes its own detectability, and is designed for authorized operators: deployment adds authentication, role-based access and an audit trail (Phase 1).
